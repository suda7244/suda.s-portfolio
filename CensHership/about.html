Our Mission

Why we made this project

We created CensHERship because there is such a big gap between how platforms describe moderation and how it actually feels. What is framed as neutral or protective can end up feeling like disappearance, reduced visibility, or pressure to stay quiet.

We wanted to bring those experiences together and make them harder to ignore. Once these stories sit alongside survey findings and design work, it becomes clearer that they are not isolated moments, but part of a wider pattern.

“This project is not only about censorship. It is about whose voices remain visible.”

Why It Matters

What is lost when health content is pushed out of view

◌

Visibility

When women's health content is hidden or pushed down, important conversations become harder to find, follow, and share.

✱

Language

People stop saying things directly and start changing their words just to stay visible.

—

Access

When visibility drops, access drops too — to support, information, shared knowledge, and care.
